COMPANY LOGO
============
The included company-logo.svg is a neutral placeholder.

To use the actual company logo:
1. Replace assets/company-logo.svg with your approved company logo SVG, or
2. Change the <img> source in ui/index.html to company-logo.png.

APP ICON
========
favicon.svg is the browser-style application favicon.
app.ico is the Windows desktop icon.

The icon is intentionally a custom EnergyAutomation energy-bolt design,
not a copy of the Google Chrome logo.
